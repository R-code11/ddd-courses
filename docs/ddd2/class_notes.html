  ggplot2::ggplot(ggplot2::aes(x = ID18C, y = COPC)) +
  ggplot2::geom_smooth(se = T) + 
  ggplot2::labs(
    x = "Years of Education",
    y = "Consumption per capita",
    title = "Scatter Plot of Education and Consumption with Smooth Line"
  )
# se is used to add confidence interval around the smooth line, if se is false then there will be no confidence interval around the smooth line. The confidence interval gives us an idea of the uncertainty around the smooth line, it shows us the range of values within which we can be confident that the true relationship between the two variables lies. If the confidence interval is narrow, it means that we can be more confident about the relationship between the two variables, if it is wide, it means that we are less confident about the relationship between the two variables.

# THESE ARE THE CORELATIONS , BETWEEN , EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION, AND HOW LAND OWNERSHIP AFFECTS THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION. THE SMOOTH LINE SHOWS US THE TREND OF THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION, IF THE LINE IS UPWARD SLOPING THEN IT MEANS THAT THERE IS A POSITIVE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION, IF IT IS DOWNWARD SLOPING THEN IT MEANS THAT THERE IS A NEGATIVE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION. THE COLOR OF THE LINE SHOWS US THE DIFFERENCE IN THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION FOR LAND OWNERS AND NON-LAND OWNERS. IF THE LINES ARE SEPARATE THEN IT MEANS THAT THERE IS A DIFFERENCE IN THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION FOR LAND OWNERS AND NON-LAND OWNERS, IF THEY ARE OVERLAPPING THEN IT MEANS THAT THERE IS NO DIFFERENCE IN THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EDUCATION AND CONSUMPTION FOR LAND OWNERS AND NON-LAND OWNERS. 
#  THE CORELATION IS POSITIVE FOR BOTH LAND OWNERS AND NON-LAND OWNERS, BUT THE CORELATION IS STRONGER FOR LAND OWNERS THAN NON-LAND OWNERS, BECAUSE THE SMOOTH LINE FOR LAND OWNERS IS MORE UPWARD SLOPING THAN THE SMOOTH LINE FOR NON-LAND OWNERS. THIS MEANS THAT EDUCATION HAS A STRONGER IMPACT ON CONSUMPTION FOR LAND OWNERS THAN NON-LAND OWNERS.
